Who we are

A joint collaboration between WaterPlus and STRATEGY369

WaterX Asia is a joint initiative between WaterPlus, a China-based digital water platform focused on China's water industry, and STRATEGY369 Consulting, a Malaysia-based firm advising on policy, regulation and commercial strategy across ASEAN. WaterPlus brings first-hand knowledge of Chinese water utilities, technologies and suppliers, while STRATEGY369 brings a clear understanding of how water projects are planned, regulated and procured in Malaysia and the wider region. Together, we are building a trusted bridge between Chinese water technology and the utilities, buyers, regulators and investors who need it, bringing market intelligence, reviewed English-language company profiles and confidential partnership opportunities into one place, starting with Malaysia and ASEAN.

What we do

We curate water-sector market intelligence, including tenders, policy updates, data and thought leadership, and turn Chinese supplier information into clear, English-language company profiles and opportunity listings.

Why it matters

Many capable Chinese water-technology companies are well established at home but hard to find, assess or contact from elsewhere in Asia. Language barriers and limited English information slow down procurement and partnerships.

How we work

AI speeds up translation, drafting and design so content can be produced quickly and at scale. Every profile is then checked by a Mandarin-speaking reviewer before it goes live, so it stays accurate and true to the original source.

What the numbers say

Investment concentration: Indonesia and Vietnam together account for over half of ASEAN's identified water and wastewater pipeline, driven by urban population growth and PPP reform.
Efficiency gap: non-revenue water exceeds 35% in the Philippines and Indonesia — more than four times Singapore's sub-9% benchmark, and the region's single biggest efficiency opportunity.
Next wave: wastewater treatment coverage (61% average) lags drinking-water access (92% average) across most of ASEAN, pointing to where the next investment cycle is likely to land.
