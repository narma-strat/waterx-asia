Who we are

A joint collaboration between WaterPlus and STRAT369

WaterPlus brings China's water-sector knowledge, technology and supplier ecosystem. STRAT369 brings Malaysian and ASEAN policy, regulatory and commercial advisory expertise.

Together, WaterX Asia is being built as the trusted layer between Chinese water technology and buyers, regulators and investors across Asia — starting with Malaysia and ASEAN.

What we do — curate market intelligence and turn Chinese supplier information into English-language commercial assets, at scale, using AI.
Why it matters — Chinese suppliers are well known at home but largely invisible to English-speaking buyers across Asia.
How we work — AI handles first-pass translation, content and design; a Mandarin-speaking reviewer is the single quality gate before anything goes live.

What the numbers say

Investment concentration: Indonesia and Vietnam together account for over half of ASEAN's identified water and wastewater pipeline, driven by urban population growth and PPP reform.
Efficiency gap: non-revenue water exceeds 35% in the Philippines and Indonesia — more than four times Singapore's sub-9% benchmark, and the region's single biggest efficiency opportunity.
Next wave: wastewater treatment coverage (61% average) lags drinking-water access (92% average) across most of ASEAN, pointing to where the next investment cycle is likely to land.
